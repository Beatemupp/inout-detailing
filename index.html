Privacy & refunds

The short, plain version.

Your info

When you book, message us or make an account, we keep your name, phone, email (if you give it), the address where your car is, your car details, and any notes or messages you send.

We use it only to book, confirm and do your detail, send you updates about your booking, and answer your messages. Only the owner of In & Out Detailing sees it. We never sell it or share it for ads.

A few trusted services help run the site: Supabase stores bookings and accounts, Brevo sends our emails, Stripe handles card payments, and OpenStreetMap's free Photon service suggests addresses as you type. We never see or keep your card number.

Reviews you post are public, with the name you type. Want your info or account deleted? Email us and it's gone.

Prices & paying

The price on the site is an estimate. The owner confirms the final price after seeing the car, and there are no surprise charges. You pay after the job. A 10% deposit, when offered, is optional and comes off your bill.

Changes, cancels & refunds

Need to move or cancel? Tap Change or cancel in the 💬 chat, or email us. If you cancel, any deposit you paid is refunded.

Not happy with something? Tell us. We have NO problem making it right, fast and fitting. The In & Out Way.
